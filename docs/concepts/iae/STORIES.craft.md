# Stories: craft

The story-building tools around the prose.

- As an author, I can track plot.
- As an author, I can track tempo.
- As an author, I can track truths about characters.
- As an author, I can see a drama-visualization graph of the work -- the
  shape of the story, Vonnegut-style.
- As an author, I can keep knowledge bases, scene flow, dramatic notes,
  storyboards, and act structure for the work.
- As an author, I can fill any of those in by hand.
- As an author, I can have an AI help me fill any of those in.
- As an author, I can start from disjointed high points -- scenes,
  stories, reveals that make the story interesting -- before I know where
  they go.
- As an author, I can arrange those high points on a timeline.
- As an author, I can keep the chronological timeline -- what happened,
  cause and effect -- separate from the narrative timeline.
- As an author, I can tell the story out of order -- flashback,
  flashforward, drug-induced amnesia, a literally untrustworthy narrator
  -- and the narrative timeline decides what lands on the page.
