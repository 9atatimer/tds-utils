# Stories: setup

Getting set up by talking to an AI.

- As a new author, I can get set up by having a conversation with an AI.
- As a new author, the AI sets all the details; I do not configure them.
- As a new author, I come out of the setup conversation with an act
  structure.
- As a new author, I come out of it with character bibles and settings
  notes.
- As a new author, I come out of it with an outline and scene notes.
- As a new author, I come out of it with what is wanted and what to
  avoid.
- As a new author, I come out of it with the language and tone set.
- As a new author, I come out of it with a description of my audience.
