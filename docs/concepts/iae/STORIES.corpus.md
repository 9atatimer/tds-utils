# Stories: corpus

The many files, of many formats, that cut across the writing.

- As an author, I can keep emotional pacing charts alongside the
  manuscript.
- As an author, I can keep tone maps alongside the manuscript.
- As an author, I can keep character bibles and scene bibles alongside
  the manuscript.
- As an author, I can keep skills and prompts for the AI as part of the
  work.
- As an author, I can keep piles of reference material and tone and voice
  examples where the work can reach them.
- As an author, I can keep pictures and video with the work. (a "maybe")
- As an author, I can see how these files cut across the writing, not
  just list them.
- As an author, I can add a corpus of my prior works for the IAE to be
  aware of.
