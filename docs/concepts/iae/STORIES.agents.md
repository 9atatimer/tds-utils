# Stories: agents

Agents you ask, and agents that watch: background and foreground, with
one kind of notification for all.

- As an author, I can ask an agent to do something for me.
- As an author, I am toasted by a watching agent when something I wrote
  contradicts something already established.
- As an author, I am told when a character says something they could not
  say because they do not know it.
- As a watching agent, I observe the author's work and speak up with
  observations, not rewrites.
- As an author, I have background AIs and analyzers working over my
  writing without my asking.
- As an author, I have foreground AIs and analyzers working with me on
  what I am doing now.
- As an author, I can have real-time specialists in the foreground while
  I write.
- As an author, I get notifications the same way no matter which AI,
  analyzer, or specialist raised them.
