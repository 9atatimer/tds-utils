# Stories: reviewing

> Part of [CONCEPT.md](CONCEPT.md). Phase 1 -- not acceptance criteria.

- As a PR author, I can read `ocr`'s findings on the PR, at the lines they
  are about.
- As Todd, I never mistake a review that reviewed nothing for a clean one:
  no false negatives. A review that reviewed nothing fails, fast and loud.
- As Todd, I am told fast and loud when a review could not reach my laptop
  or its model -- the tunnel is down, the internet is off, or the laptop is
  asleep -- instead of waiting on a review that will never come.
- As an LLM triaging a review, I can read its findings in a form I can act
  on.
- As Todd, I can see over time how often `ocr`'s findings turn out to be
  right, so I can decide whether to keep it.
- As Todd, I can see over time how often reviews fail or are interrupted,
  and why.
