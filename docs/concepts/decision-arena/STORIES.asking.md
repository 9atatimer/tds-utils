# Stories: asking

> Part of [CONCEPT.md](CONCEPT.md). Phase 1 -- not acceptance criteria.

- As the ocrinator, I can ask whether a page boundary falls mid-entry once,
  looking at both pages, and get a probability rather than two answers that
  disagree.
- As the ocrinator, I can ask what kind of page a scan is and get a
  confidence I can set a threshold on.
- As Todd, I can run a whole corpus of page decisions overnight on my own
  laptop, without paying per call and without the scans leaving it.
- As a caller off the laptop, I can put a decision question to the arena
  the same way I reach ollama through the gateway.
- As Todd, I know nobody without a credential can reach the arena from
  outside.
- As a caller, I learn at once when the arena is down, instead of hanging.
- As Todd, I can swap the model in the arena for a better one without
  changing any caller. (Aspirational: one model to start.)
